# Communities: Keep Learning With Others

Learning AI alone is hard. These communities offer meetups, mentorship, competitions and opportunities, from Juba to the rest of Africa and beyond.

---

## In South Sudan

### Scenius Hub Tech Community

The organisers of IndabaX South Sudan. Scenius Hub runs regular tech meetups, industry sessions with professionals, hands-on workshops and community events in Juba.

- **What you get:** In-person meetups, industry sessions (software engineering, fintech, AI), workshops, a library and a co-working space with reliable internet.
- **How to join:** Follow Scenius Hub's events at [sceniushub.com/event-list](https://www.sceniushub.com/event-list) and on its social media pages, and attend the next meetup.
- **Website:** [sceniushub.com](https://www.sceniushub.com)

### IndabaX South Sudan

The South Sudan chapter of the Deep Learning Indaba movement, organised by Scenius Hub. Held for the first time in July 2025 and again in 2026 with meetups, a hackathon and a main event day.

- **How to take part:** Watch Scenius Hub's channels for calls for speakers, volunteers and participants for the next edition.
- **Ways to contribute:** Volunteer as an organiser, give a talk, mentor at a meetup, or contribute to the open-source food security forecasting project.

## Across Africa

### Deep Learning Indaba

Africa's largest machine learning gathering, with an annual flagship event that rotates between African countries, plus IndabaX chapters in many countries. South Sudan first took part as an IndabaX chapter in 2025.

- **What you get:** Scholarships to attend the main event, research talks, mentorship, awards and a continent-wide network.
- **Website:** [deeplearningindaba.com](https://deeplearningindaba.com/)

### Zindi

Africa's data science competition platform, with tens of thousands of data scientists across the continent. Zindi hosted the IndabaX South Sudan 2026 hackathon.

- **What you get:** Competitions on African problems with cash prizes, learning challenges for beginners, job opportunities and a public ranking that shows your skills.
- **How to start:** Create a free account, join a **Knowledge** or beginner-friendly competition first, read the starter notebook, then submit. Your first goal is a submission, not a top score.
- **Website:** [zindi.africa](https://zindi.africa/)
- **Local contact:** Zindi has a Country Ambassador in South Sudan; ask at Scenius Hub meetups.

### Masakhane

A grassroots research community focused on natural language processing for African languages, built by and for Africans.

- **What you get:** Open research projects you can contribute to, even as a beginner; a strong fit if you want to work on South Sudanese languages.
- **Website:** [masakhane.io](https://www.masakhane.io/)

### Data Science Africa

A network that organises schools and workshops on data science and machine learning across Africa.

- **Website:** [datascienceafrica.org](https://www.datascienceafrica.org/)

## How to get the most out of a community

1. **Show up consistently.** Attending regularly matters more than attending everything.
2. **Ask questions.** Everyone was a beginner once, and good questions help the whole group.
3. **Share what you build,** even small projects. Present at a meetup or post on GitHub.
4. **Help someone newer than you.** Teaching is one of the fastest ways to learn.
5. **Look out for opportunities.** Scholarships, fellowships, internships and travel grants are often shared first in communities.
