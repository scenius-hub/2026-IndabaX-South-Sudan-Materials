# Resources: Keep Learning After IndabaX South Sudan 2026

IndabaX South Sudan 2026 took participants through four stages: **Learn → Engage → Build → Showcase**. This folder is for what comes next: free courses, tools, datasets and communities to help you keep building AI and machine learning skills in South Sudan.

Everything listed here is **free** or has a free option. Most of it works on a phone or a low-spec laptop, and much of it runs in the browser with no installation.

## A suggested 12-week plan

You don't need to do everything. If you can give **5–7 hours a week**, this plan takes you from zero to your first competition submission.

| Weeks | Focus | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–3 | Python fundamentals | [courses.md → Stage 1](courses.md#stage-1-python-and-programming-basics) |
| 4–5 | Data handling with pandas, basic statistics | [courses.md → Stage 2](courses.md#stage-2-data-skills-and-maths-foundations) |
| 6–8 | Core machine learning | [courses.md → Stage 3](courses.md#stage-3-machine-learning-fundamentals), re-run the [workshop notebook](<../ML Workshop>) |
| 9–10 | A small project on a local dataset | [datasets.md](datasets.md) |
| 11–12 | Your first Zindi or Kaggle competition | [communities.md](communities.md) |

## Tips for learning in South Sudan

- **Download when the connection is good.** Many courses let you download videos, and notebooks can be saved offline.
- **Use Google Colab instead of installing software.** It runs in the browser on Google's computers, so your laptop's specs matter less. See [tools.md](tools.md).
- **Learn with others.** Study groups finish courses far more often than solo learners. Join the Scenius Hub Tech Community meetups.
- **Build something local.** Projects using South Sudanese data (rainfall, food prices, crop yields) stand out and teach you more than copying tutorials.
- **Share your work on GitHub.** It becomes your portfolio for jobs, scholarships and fellowships.
